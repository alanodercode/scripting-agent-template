## Hooks

### What Works
- **Direct identification > Questions** — "I used to..." beats "Do you...?" Identity beats interrogation.
- **Specific numbers > Vague claims** — "12 pills" beats "tons of supplements."
- **Show the pain first** — Open with the problem they're experiencing, not the solution.
- **Filter for buyer** — Hook must attract YOUR buyer, not just attention. A car hook attracts car people, not watch buyers.

### What Fails
- Generic questions ("Struggling with X?")
- Starting with the product
- Clever wordplay that doesn't identify the viewer

## Body

### What Works
- **B-roll every 2-3 seconds** — Not every 10 seconds. Non-negotiable.
- **Proof mid-body, not end** — Weave credibility in, don't dump at CTA.
- **Show don't tell** — Demo beats pitch.
- **One visual per claim** — "Diets, exercise, supplements" = 3 quick cuts, not one shot.

### What Fails
- Talking head for 8+ seconds straight
- Generic stock footage
- Proof as afterthought

## Formats

### Performance Hierarchy
1. **Demo** — Product doing the thing (best)
2. **Talking head + b-roll hybrid** — Face for trust, cuts for retention
3. **Pure UGC** — Works for casual products
4. **Pure b-roll** — Rarely works alone

### Format-Specific
- VSLs work for considered purchases, education-heavy products
- Short-form (<30s) for impulse, simple products
- Test both — some brands surprise

## Sound

- **Movement = sound** — Slide = whoosh. Pop-up = click. Always.
- **Music mirrors emotion** — Dark for problem, bright when solution appears.
- **Body sounds make pain real** — Joints = creak. Stomach = rumble.

---

## CTAs

- **Direction + Urgency + Value** — Arrow, timer/deadline, discount. Missing any = fewer clicks.
- **Show the destination** — Screenshot of checkout reduces friction.
- **Trust signal in CTA** — Regulator, review count, money-back guarantee.

---

## Audiences

- **Counter-intuitive wins** — Always test unexpected demographics.
- **Don't assume** — Test before excluding.

---

## Creative Principles

- **Sell the vacation, not the flight** — Outcome > features.
- **Ugly but clear > Pretty but confusing** — Message clarity beats production value.
- **Real > Polished** — Authentic/organic outperforms overproduced.
