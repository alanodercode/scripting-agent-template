# Writing Guide
*Universal style rules. Apply to ALL scripts unless brand overrides.*

---

## Core Rule: Spoken Word Only

Scripts are read aloud by humans. They must sound like someone talking, not copywriting.

### Forbidden
```
❌ Pures Glas. Kein Kratzen. Terpene pur.
❌ Statement. Statement. Statement.
❌ Em dashes for drama — like this
❌ Hashtag style #NoMorePills
❌ ALL CAPS FOR EMPHASIS
❌ "Revolutionary" / "Game-changing" / "Miracle"
```

### Required
```
✓ Flowing sentences connected with "and," "but," "so," "because"
✓ Contractions: "it's," "don't," "you're" (not "it is," "do not")
✓ Humanifiers (see below)
✓ One thought per sentence
```

---

## Humanifiers

Small words that make scripts sound real:

### English
- "honestly," "basically," "actually"
- "look," "here's the thing," "so"
- "I mean," "you know," "right?"
- "like" (sparingly)

### German
- "halt," "irgendwie," "eigentlich"
- "ich mein," "weißt du," "also"
- "echt," "mega," "krass"

**Use 2–3 per script, mid-sentence.** Not every sentence. Not as a standalone opener before a routine claim (that is staging — see `humanizer.md`).

---

## Humanize Pass (Mandatory)

After the raw draft, before save: run `frameworks/humanizer.md`.

Keep claims. Kill AI shape (not-X-but-Y, staged openers, one-line closers, forced triads, inflated significance, StoryScope tidy-theme endings). Spoken-word humanifiers stay mid-sentence (2–3). Em dashes still forbidden. Do not invent proof.

Source stack: blader/humanizer + Wikipedia Signs of AI writing + StoryScope structural tells (adapted for ads).

## Read-Aloud Test

Before finishing ANY script:
1. Read it out loud
2. If you stumble, rewrite
3. If it sounds like an ad, rewrite
4. If you wouldn't say it to a friend, rewrite

---

## Hook Rules

### Structure
- First line = identification OR pattern interrupt
- Must work with sound OFF (visual must hook too)
- No "Hey guys!" or "What's up!" openings

### Length
- First sentence: Under 10 words ideal
- Hook section: 3-5 seconds spoken

---

## Body Rules

### Pacing
- Short paragraphs (2-3 sentences max)
- Vary sentence length
- Break after emotional beats

### Transitions
- "So here's what happened..."
- "And that's when I realized..."
- "But here's the thing..."

NOT:
- "Furthermore..."
- "Additionally..."
- "In conclusion..."

---

## CTA Rules

### Always Include
- What to do ("tap the link")
- Where it goes ("in my bio" / "below")
- Why now (urgency or offer)

### Never
- Multiple CTAs competing
- Vague "check it out"
- Missing the actual action

---

## Numbers

- Write out one through ten
- Use digits for 11+
- Use digits for impact: "12 pills" not "twelve pills"
- Round for credibility: "350,000+" not "347,892"

---

## Compliance Defaults

Unless brand specifies otherwise:
- No "cure" / "treat" / "heal" for health products
- No income claims without disclaimers
- No "guaranteed results"
- Add "results may vary" for transformations
