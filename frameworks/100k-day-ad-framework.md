# The $100k/Day Ad Framework — Execution Instructions

## Objective
Create ads that scale profitably by building belief, attention, and hope before revealing the product — using sequencing and psychology, not flashy editing.

## Core Principle (Do This First)

Never follow this outdated structure:
> Hook → Product Reveal → Benefits → CTA

Instead, always follow this sequence:
> **Emotional Hook (Open Loop) → Call Out the Right Audience → Build Belief via a Unique Mechanism → Reveal the Product**

Each step must earn the right to move to the next.

---

## Step 1: Create a Scroll-Stopping Hook (Non-Negotiable)

Your hook must do at least ONE of the following:

1. **Call out the avatar**
   - "Gym owners…"
   - "If you're over 35…"

2. **Call out the problem**
   - "Your metabolism after 35 is lying to you"

3. **Call out the solution**
   - "This Japanese morning ritual changed fat loss"

**Instruction:**
- Choose ONE of the three angles above
- Make it obvious in the first 1–2 seconds
- You may layer engagement (humor, shock, randomness) after the call-out (e.g. Alex Hormozi riding a unicorn after saying "Are you a gym owner?")

✅ If the right person doesn't instantly think "this is for me", the ad is dead.

---

## Step 2: Open an Emotional Loop Immediately

People do not buy in low emotional states. They buy when they feel **hope**.

**Instruction:**
- Start with tension, curiosity, or contrast
- Create a question the viewer needs answered

Examples of open loops:
- "I stepped outside and saw something I didn't expect…"
- "I tried this thinking it wouldn't work — then this happened"
- "Everyone told me this was impossible after 35…"

Do not resolve the loop immediately.

✅ Humans crave closure. If there's no unanswered question, they scroll.

---

## Step 3: DO NOT Reveal the Product Yet

**Common Mistake to Avoid:**
"Struggling with X? Try Product Y!" — This kills belief instantly.

**Instruction:**
- Delay the product reveal
- First, explain why what they've tried before didn't work
- Introduce a new way of thinking before introducing your solution

Think: "The reason nothing has worked for you isn't discipline — it's mechanism."

This is how long VSLs convert: They build belief first, then sell.

---

## Step 4: Introduce a Unique Mechanism (Belief Builder)

A unique mechanism is a new explanation for how the result is achieved.

**Same result + new mechanism = renewed belief.**

**Instruction:**
- Identify what your product actually does
- Reframe it as a distinct method, not a generic feature
- Make it feel new, specific, and different

Ways to Create a New Mechanism:
- **New form factor** — Powder → Gummies
- **New concentration or spec** — "30% higher extract"
- **New origin story** — "Sourced from Japanese Highlands"
- **New technical framing** — "Nano-ground particles small enough to penetrate cell walls"

Even if competitors have the same thing — they're not framing it this way.

✅ New mechanisms reset skepticism and create hope.

---

## Step 5: Funnel Them Toward the Product Reveal

Only after:
- The hook stops the scroll
- The open loop creates curiosity
- The mechanism builds belief

…do you reveal the product.

**Instruction:**
- Position the product as the **vehicle** for the mechanism
- Not "buy this"
- But "this is how you access the mechanism"

Example: "That's why we created ___ — the only [mechanism] designed to ___."

Now belief exists → conversions follow.
